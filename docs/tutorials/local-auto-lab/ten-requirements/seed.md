# JobDigest: the seed

Paste the paragraph below when the interview asks you to describe your product. Tell ViD it is a **seed**: your own demand, kept word for word.

---

JobDigest, the smallest version. A visitor saves one job search with their email address, confirms it from a link in an email, and from then on gets one email a day listing the new jobs that match. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link and a support address. Jobs come from one feed. Nothing else: no accounts, no passwords, no mobile app, no second feed, no matching by other names of a job, no admin screens.

---

## Why the seed is this small

The seed sets the size of everything after it. A first try with a three-sentence seed ("two feeds merged into one store, synonym-aware search, an email digest per saved search") grew to 47 requirements at the second approval and 64 at the third, most of them from sensible defaults ViD proposed and the answers accepted. This seed names what is out as plainly as what is in, and the answers in `answers.md` choose less wherever a question offers more.

## The product manager's stance at every sitting

- Where a question offers more or less scope, choose less.
- Where ViD proposes something the seed does not ask for, say no, or "later".
- At the first approval, if ViD has more than ten requirements, ask it in plain words to cut to ten and move the rest out of this version.
