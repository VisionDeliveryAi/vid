# JobDigest search: the seed

Paste the paragraph below when ViD asks what you want to build. Tell it this is **your demand**: your own brief, kept word for word, which outranks everything else.

---

JobDigest search, the smallest thing that is useful. One page. A visitor types one or more words and sees the jobs whose titles contain every one of those words, as whole words, upper or lower case alike. Each job shows its title, its employer, its location and a link to the posting. When nothing matches, the page says so. The jobs come from a list that ships with the product. Nothing else: no accounts, no email, no saved searches, no feed to read, no admin screens, nothing stored about the visitor.

---

## Why the seed is this small

The seed sets the size of everything after it. This one names what is out as plainly as what is in, so that the whole thing, from the interview to a built and judged product, fits in an afternoon. A larger first version of the same idea (saved searches, a confirmation email, a daily email) is in `ten-requirements/`: ten requirements, a little over an hour of interview and more than four hours for its first thin slice alone.

## The product manager's stance at every sitting

- Where a question offers more or less, choose less.
- Where ViD proposes something the seed does not ask for, say "later".
- At the first approval, say the size in words: at most three requirements.
