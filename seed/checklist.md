# Seed checklist lines — the promotion target (§7.2, §7.3)

Read by `vid init` when seeding a project's `GRADING-CHECKLIST.md`.
Judgment-only failure modes — the ones that leave no countable artifact.

**This file ships empty.** It grows only by promotion: checklist lines the
owner marks general at run end land here, stack-neutrally formulated. A
seeded line that fires nothing across two runs is dropped (the complexity
law). The harness-attack standing line ships in
`templates/GRADING-CHECKLIST.md` itself; a project's domain lines come from
its interview and this library.

Entry format:

    - <question a grader must answer>. | scope: <universal | domain: <genus>> | evidence: <run/finding>
